- **Fixed**: a safe POV's write-ahead log could wedge for good with its ring almost empty of usable room ([#959](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/959)). An update in a POV holds the ring's head until a promotion carries it into a root, and that promotion writes a record of its own; once other records had filled the ring up to the head, no promotion could get room, so the head could never move. The ring now keeps room for every pending promotion from the moment the update's record is written: a record for each level the update still has to climb, sized from the record that pushed it. Other writes are refused (`insufficient_storage`, as before) rather than take that room, and a promotion may spend the room kept for the updates it carries. The room is freed when the promotion lands, when a POV is discarded, or when a reservation goes unused, and recovery owes it again for the updates the replay leaves unpromoted. A paused POV still holds the head while it stays paused. The WAL and checkpoint formats are unchanged.
