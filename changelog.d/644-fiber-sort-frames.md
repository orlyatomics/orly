- **Fixed**: `orly/indy/fiber/algorithm.test` no longer dies at random (about 1 suite round in 60 on aarch64, exit 1 with no summary). `Fiber::Sort` freed each sub-sort's frame as soon as the last sub-sort reported done, but a sub-sort reports done before its fiber has switched off the frame. A frame freed under its own fiber killed the test in two ways: in debug, `Cannot Free Frame that is still running` and a terminate; or, when the test then tore down its frame pool, a fiber still finishing its switch on an unmapped stack, which is a SIGSEGV that no handler can report. Each sub-sort now has its runner free its frame once it has switched off it (`FreeMyFrame`), and the test joins its worker runners before tearing down the frame pools. `Fiber::Sort` is used only by this test (#644).
