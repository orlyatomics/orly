- **Fixed**: building an update whose entries span several indexes took O(N²) time in the number of entries: entries were linked in index-id order, and each one walked back past those already linked to find its place by key. An update's entries are now sorted by key and linked once, in `TUpdate`'s constructor, and the write, Tetris-peek, slave-import and replay paths build theirs that way. At 4,096 entries over 4–8 indexes, building the update goes from 63–80 ms to about 1.3 ms ([#879](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/879)).
