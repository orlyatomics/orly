- **Fixed**: a paused POV with writes no longer holds the WAL head at those writes until it is unpaused (#963). When the oldest of them falls more than half the ring behind, the checkpointer copies all of the POV's unpromoted updates, with its pause, forward into a new `RepoCopy` record (sooner, once it is more than a sixteenth of the ring behind, if the ring has refused a record since the last checkpoint and the copies would give back a sixteenth of the ring more than they write; Pov and pause records holding the head are then copied with it), and once that copy is durable it lets the head pass the originals. Recovery rebuilds the POV from the newest complete copy and replays the records after it as before. Slaves copy their own. The room that copies need is kept for them, like promotion room (#959), and paused POVs together may hold at most `(C - C/8 - C/16)/2 - live` of a ring of C bytes, where `live` is what the ring holds from the oldest unpromoted update of a POV that isn't paused (so no copy can move it), and nothing once that is negative: a write into a paused POV, or a pause, that would take them past that is refused as retriable (`TWalPausedFull`), and nothing else is. A copy that isn't durable within 5 s is not written again; the next checkpoint finishes it.
- **Changed (on-disk format, one-way)**: the first copy marks the store with a new incompatible feature (superblock format version 3, `IncompatFeatures` bit `WalRepoCopy`). Binaries from before this change refuse to open such a store rather than lose a paused POV's writes; stores that never paused a POV for long are unchanged and stay readable by older binaries. `orly_dm` will not downgrade it.
- **Fixed**: pausing or unpausing a safe POV now returns only once its WAL record is durable, as creating one does. Before, a crash just after the call returned could bring the POV back with its old status (`tests/pause_restart_test.sh`'s `no-ring-empty` scenario, which pauses an empty POV and kills the server at once, failed on a fast enough machine).
- **Fixed**: a WAL stopped with records still queued and every in-flight group slot taken, or with auto-seal off and synced records not yet sealed, no longer spins holding its lock on shutdown; it waits for the syncer to free a slot and seals what is synced before it stops. The syncer also keeps running until the leader has stopped, not just until it is told to stop: before, a WAL stopped while it synced its only in-flight group, with more queued than one group holds, left the leader waiting for good for a syncer that had gone.
- **Fixed**: rewriting a device's superblock (`orly_dm`, and marking a store with an incompatible feature) now fails if the write is short or the `fsync` fails, instead of ignoring the `fsync` result. A store whose mark didn't reach the device is not given copies.
