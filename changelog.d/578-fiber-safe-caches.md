- **Fixed**: on linux/arm64 the release `orlyi` (including the `v0.1.0` image) segfaulted right after the first write through a POV. `TSafeRepo::RemoveFile` switches onto each scheduler to clear its per-thread read-file cache, but GCC had read the thread pointer once at the top of that function, so after the switch it dereferenced the *calling* thread's `TLocalReadFileCache::Cache`, which is null. It is the #554 hazard on a thread-local #555 left unaudited. `TLocalReadFileCache::Cache`, `TLocalWalkerCache::Cache` and `TDiskController::TEvent::LocalEventPool` are now `TFiberSafeLocal`, the same as the fiber runner's own locals. A native arm64 loop went from 10/10 crashes to 0/20. amd64 was never affected, because x86-64 re-derives the TLS base at every access (#578).
