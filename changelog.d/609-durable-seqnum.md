- **Fixed**: sessions and POVs saved after a restart no longer lose to the copies saved before it. The durable store numbers every save and keeps, on load and on merge, the copy with the highest number, but after a restart it numbered new saves from 1 again. A session saved before a restart with number 500 and again after it with number 2 therefore loaded as the old copy once the new one left the cache, and the next durable merge discarded the new copy for good. On reopen, the store now reads every entry's header in its files and resumes numbering above the highest one. That is one pass over the durable files at startup, which merging keeps to about one copy of each live session and POV, and it is logged with its duration. Existing stores need nothing: the numbers were always on disk (#609).
