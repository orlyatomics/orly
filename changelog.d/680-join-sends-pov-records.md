- **Fixed**: a pov made before a slave joined couldn't be used after a failover. Its durable record reached a slave only if the pov was saved again while the two were paired, because the join sent the master's live repos but not the povs' records, so the promoted slave refused the pov (`durable object doesn't exist`) although it had its repo. The join now sends the saved record of each pov whose repo it inventories, in batches of at most 1024 records or 1 MiB, before the slave syncs. They travel as durable saves on the live stream's existing message, so a slave from before this change stores them too. Sessions still reach a slave only when saved while paired; reads through a pov don't need them. Covered by `import_replication.test`'s new `JoinSendsPovRecords`.
