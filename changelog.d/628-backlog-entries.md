- **Fixed**: the writer backpressure now caps a POV's unpromoted backlog in entries as well as updates, at 1/32 of the Update Entry pool. A POV's memory merge copies its whole backlog, entries included. A batch is one update with an entry per write, so with batches the cap in updates (from #586) let one POV's backlog hold most of the Entry pool, and memory admission (#607) refused writers at the reserve. Measured on native arm64 with 8 writers sending 200-write batches (20,000/40,000 pools, the default 25% reserve, 30 s): to one POV, refusals fell from about 3,500 to 0 and the Entry pool's peak from about 30,000 to 12,000, at the same throughput (173 batches/s against 167); with a POV per writer, refusals fell from about 4,400 to about 700. With 32 POVs all backlogged, their caps add up to the whole pool, and nothing changes. A new CI smoke, `clients/smoke/run-batch-backlog.sh`, sends the batches to one POV and requires no refusal, no pool miss and an Entry pool under half full (#628).
