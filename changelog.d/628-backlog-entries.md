- **Fixed**: the writer backpressure now caps a POV's unpromoted backlog in entries as well as updates, at 1/32 of the Update Entry pool. A POV's memory merge copies its whole backlog, entries included. A batch is one update with an entry per write, so with batches the cap in updates (from #586) let one POV's backlog hold most of the Entry pool, and the merges and Tetris ran out of entries while writers were refused at the memory reserve (#607). Measured on native arm64 with 8 writers sending 200-write batches to one POV (20,000/40,000 pools, 25% reserve, 30 s): before, 117 to 138 Entry-pool misses, about 3,300 refused batches and the Entry pool peaking at 37,500 to 39,600; after, no miss, no refusal, a peak of 12,600, and the same throughput (165 to 170 batches/s against 161 to 163). With a POV per writer, misses fell from about 300 to between 0 and 6 and refusals from about 4,200 to about 700. With 32 POVs all backlogged at once, their caps add up to the whole pool, so this changes nothing there: at a 25% reserve both builds had about 150 merge and Tetris misses in 30 s (each one retried, not fatal), at 35% between 0 and 8, and at 50% none. A new CI smoke, `clients/smoke/run-batch-backlog.sh`, sends the batches with a 25% reserve and requires no refusal, no pool miss and an Entry pool under half full (#628).
