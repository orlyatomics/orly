- **Added**: `orlyi --device=<name or glob>` (repeatable) limits the volume scan to the devices named, and `orly_dm --scan=<name or glob>` does the same for `--list` and the scan it does before `--create-volume`. Globs are `fnmatch(3)` patterns (`loop3`, `/dev/ram*`). Several byte copies of one volume (same instance name, a dd'd image on RAM disks or loop devices) can now be opened in parallel on one host, one `orlyi` per copy. Without `--device`, `orlyi` now refuses to start on two devices that are copies of one volume and names both, instead of picking between them; `orly_dm --list` lists such copies instead of failing an assertion (#970).
- **Fixed**: a device without `queue/rq_affinity`, such as a `brd` RAM disk, no longer aborts the volume scan; it is logged at info level and used (#970).
