- **Fixed**: every restart removed and rewrote the saved-repo entries of the safe POVs that WAL recovery restores (#966). The restart sweep of #671 noted every entry in the system repo before WAL recovery ran, so the first restored POV's creation removed the entries of all the others, and each one's restore then wrote its own back. On a store with 2,116 open safe POVs every open logged the same ~2,100 removals and repeated them on the next. The sweep now runs after WAL recovery and leaves alone the entries of repos that are live by then; the startup log line says how many of the entries that came back belong to live repos. `tests/saved_repo_restart_test.sh` checks it across two reopens.
