- **Fixed**: `orly/indy/fault_injection.test` intermittently reported a merge as hanging when an injected sync failed (#682). The merge wasn't hanging. It aborted, as merges do by design after an I/O error, but GitHub's runners pipe every core dump to `systemd-coredump`, and the child isn't reaped until the dump is written. On a loaded x86 runner that took over 5 s per abort, so some aborts ran past the harness's 6 s deadline and were killed as hangs. The harness's children no longer dump core, which brings an abort under 0.5 s under the same load. A child killed at the deadline is now reported with its `/proc` state (so a slow core dump can't pass for a hang again), and each abort's duration is printed.
