- **Added**: `--edit_runner=fast|slow|spill` (default `fast`, unchanged behaviour) chooses where a write's commit runs. A statement still runs its method on a fast runner; once it knows it has effects, `slow` moves the commit (resolving effects, the meta record, the update and the push into the memory layer, about 85% of a write's cost on the fast cores) to the least-loaded slow runner and back, and `spill` does so only while that runner has fewer than `--edit_spill_load` (default 1) commits on it. Commit order, durability and where the statement frees its frame are unchanged. In a QUICK ABBA under a 4,096-op batch ingest, `slow` raised mixed reads at 64 sessions by 11–13% and cut the open-loop 5,000/s lag p99 by 39–42%, at the cost of a slower ingest: Edit mean +8%, max up to 1.03 s, 7–8% fewer Edits in the window ([#1038](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/1038)).
