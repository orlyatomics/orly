- **Fixed**: a graceful stop under heavy write load could hang until it was killed, because two disk merge runners were livelocked removing neighbouring entries from the page cache's LRU. The LRU is an atomic list, and its `Remove()` and `Insert()` avoid deadlock by releasing a lock and retrying when a second lock is taken. The retry re-took the lock straight away, while the line was still in its own cache, so the neighbour spinning on that lock almost never got it, and that neighbour held the lock the retry needed. A retry now backs off (doubling pauses, then a yield) before taking the lock again, and spin waits read the lock before trying to take it. In a stress test on GitHub's x86 and arm runners, the old list stalled a thread for up to 4 s on x86 and over 5 s on arm, and the new one never stalled for 100 ms; contended throughput rose 1.5–1400×. A new `atomic_unordered_list.test` case has threads unlinking each other's neighbours and aborts if any thread is still spinning 10 seconds after being told to stop (#875).
