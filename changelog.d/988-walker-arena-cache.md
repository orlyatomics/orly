- **Changed**: a disk arena's stream now keeps 8 pages in its own cache instead of 128. The cache's slots were allocated with the arena whether used or not, and every file walker holds two arenas, so a walker took about 34 KiB of heap; it now takes about 4 KiB. With a 6 GiB memory budget, a read ladder of 1 to 256 sessions over about 400 disk files peaked at 10.6 GiB RSS and now peaks at 7.4 to 7.5 GiB, and its throughput went up 12 to 20% from 16 sessions on. The peak is still over the budget: the walkers in use are not bounded and merge working memory is not in the memory plan (#988).
