- **Fixed**: `orlyi` and `orlyc` now shut down gracefully on SIGTERM, as they already did on SIGINT. `RunUntilCtrlC` blocked every signal and waited with only SIGINT unblocked, so a SIGTERM stayed pending forever. `docker stop`, Kubernetes and systemd all send SIGTERM, and they then waited out their timeout and SIGKILLed the server, so the flush of dirty memory layers (#440), the durable writer's final drain (#277) and the client drain (#460) never ran. The image `exec`s `orlyi`, so this hit every container. `tests/restart_test.sh` now stops the server with SIGTERM on its first cycle and fails unless every shutdown completes on its own, where before it fell back to `kill -9` silently (#598).
