- **Fixed (data loss)**: a batched call (`callBatch`/`callMany` and their Python and Go twins, even a batch of one) to a method that evaluates an `if`, in an effect or in a value, replied `ok` and then lost its writes. Tetris replays an update's calls to test its predicate results before promoting it, and replayed a batch as one call of its first method, with args named `0.k`, `1.k`, ... that the method never looks up; the replay threw (`unordered_map::at`), the update was never promoted, and after 10 tries its POV was failed, refusing later writes. Tetris now replays each call the batch records, in order, with its own method and args. The batch's meta record now also counts its calls (`$calls`), so a batch of calls without args replays correctly, and a batch whose record wouldn't replay as the calls that ran is refused (`exception`) before it commits instead of acknowledged. Batches recorded by v0.2.0 still replay. A new smoke (`clients/smoke/run-batch-promotion.sh`, Python, TS and Go drivers) checks batched conditional, assert-only and plain writes of sizes 1, 2 and 8, same-method and mixed, on all four POV flavours after promotion, and that each POV still takes writes; the kill campaign adds a conditional-batch writer and a mixed-batch writer, and treats a failed POV as a violation (#751).
