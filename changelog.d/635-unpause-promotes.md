- **Fixed**: writes made to a POV while it was paused never reached its parent after `unpause`, unless another write to the POV followed (#635). Unpausing rejoined the parent's Tetris player only when the POV had *no* pending writes, a check that had been inverted since 2014, and a paused POV's own writes never join. Unpause now rejoins exactly when the POV has writes to promote, so they promote within milliseconds of the unpause. Unpausing an empty POV no longer joins Tetris for nothing.
