- **Fixed**: a merge that runs out of disk space no longer aborts `orlyi`. It hands its inputs back and retries after a backoff (100 ms, doubling to 3.2 s). A disk merge unmarks the two layers it took, and a memory merge puts back the layers it was flushing. Both re-queue themselves, and the merge queues are ordered by due time, so a repo that is backing off delays no other repo. Three things had to change for the retry to be safe. A failed memory-layer flush used to report every update in it as permanently failed, which freed the transactions' completion records that the retry later completes. It now leaves those reports pending. The fold step's intermediate file, left in the file map with no owner when the fold failed, is now removed. A tail that runs out of space hands its layer back and logs, instead of aborting. Writes are not refused yet when space runs low, so while the disk stays full they collect in memory until the update pool's backpressure slows them down. The durable writer and merger still abort on a full disk. The retry lines in the log are rate-limited to one every 10 s (#590).
