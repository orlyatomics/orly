- **Fixed**: `durable_manager.test`'s `SemFiresOnlyAfterFlush` no longer fails intermittently on loaded CI runners. It asserted that the durability sem has *not* fired immediately after `Save()` — sound only while less than the manager's write delay has actually elapsed, and at a 300ms delay a descheduled test thread could legitimately miss the window, so correct behaviour reported as a failure. The negative assertion is now guarded on measured elapsed time and the fixture runs at a 2s write delay, so the skip should be vanishingly rare and, when it happens, means "unprovable here" rather than "regression". The #277 property it guards is unchanged and still caught: a synchronous `Save()` push fires the sem at elapsed ≈ 0, inside the window, and fails the assertion (#551).
