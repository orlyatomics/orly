- **Fixed**: SIGTERM no longer waits for a running disk merge step to finish. `StopMergeRunners()` used to join each disk-merge loop only after its current `StepMergeDisk` returned, which with a backlog of hundreds of layers took minutes (323 s in one run, past the 120 s a harness allows before SIGKILL). The merge now checks the shutdown flag as it works through its keys and updates, frees its partial output and hands its input layers back; the inputs are untouched, so nothing is lost and the merge is queued again on the next start. Tail merges and the commutative fold pass are not interrupted (#927).
