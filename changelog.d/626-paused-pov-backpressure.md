- **Fixed**: writes to a paused POV no longer hang. A paused POV's writes aren't promoted until it is unpaused, so its backlog never drains, yet the writer backpressure from #586 made every write past the per-POV backlog cap (the Update pool / 32) wait for it to drain, for good. A reader on the same POV stalled with them. Now a write to a paused or failed POV whose backlog has reached the cap is refused before it commits, with `"status": "insufficient_memory"` and a message saying the POV is paused. Other POVs are unaffected. Letting a paused POV grow instead would have filled the update pools to the merges' reserve (#607), and every write to every POV was then refused: measured, after 1,900 writes to one paused POV. The backpressure wait now applies only to a backlog that Tetris is promoting, and gives up once the backlog hasn't shrunk for 5 s, so no write waits forever for a backlog that stopped draining. A new CI smoke, `clients/smoke/run-paused-pov.sh`, writes to a paused POV with 8 writers and a reader: before, the writers stopped at 155 writes and the reader managed 1 read in 20 s; now 155 writes go through, about 6,000 are refused, a write to another POV is accepted, and the slowest read takes 21 ms (#626).
