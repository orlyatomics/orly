- **Changed**: release builds link TCMalloc (`-ltcmalloc_minimal`, from `libgoogle-perftools-dev`; the runtime image adds `libtcmalloc-minimal4t64`). On the M1 readtrace at 64 sessions, preloading it into `orlyi` served 43% more point reads during merges and 30% more during ingest than glibc malloc, and 40–50% more joins in both, ahead of jemalloc and mimalloc on the same runs. Debug, ASan and TSan builds keep the default allocator. Building release from source now needs `libgoogle-perftools-dev` installed.
