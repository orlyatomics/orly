- **Added**: `--read_slice_us` (default 200) gives each fast runner a two-level queue. A method call that has run that long in all drops to the runner's low-priority queue, which runs only when nothing else is ready there, and from then on gives way to any statement that becomes ready; `0` keeps the single queue. In a QUICK ABBA under a batch ingest at 5,000 reads/s offered, point read p99 fell from 30–34 ms to 9–14 ms and quiet point p99 now meets its 2 ms bound, but 1,000-row range, page and xrange p99 roughly doubled, client lag p99 rose from 0.66–0.93 s to 1.48–1.56 s and quiet mixed reads at 64 sessions fell 7% ([#1015](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/1015)).
