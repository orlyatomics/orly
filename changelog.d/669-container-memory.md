- **Fixed**: `orlyi` sized its memory from the host's free RAM, ignoring a container's memory limit, and the image's entrypoint pinned 4,000 fiber frames (4 GiB of locked stacks). So the released image was OOM-killed while idle under `docker run --memory=1g` and `--memory=4g`, and idled at 6.45 GB under 8g. The budget is now the smaller of free RAM and the cgroup limit (cgroup v2 `memory.max`, including a systemd unit's or a parent slice's; v1 `memory.limit_in_bytes` as a fallback; `max` and v1's "unlimited" count as none). A new `--memory_budget_mb` sets it explicitly, and a smaller cgroup limit still caps it. Every frame count, cache and pool not given on the command line is scaled from the budget, in the same proportions as before. On a host without a limit the sizes are exactly what they were: the budget is free RAM and the scale is the same expression. A cgroup limit or `--memory_budget_mb` is treated as a total: the process baseline, the append log, sizes given explicitly and 128 MiB for the heap come out of it first. `orlyi` refuses to start, saying how much it needs, when the budget can't fund the minimum working set: those fixed costs, at least 64 frames, and Update and Update Entry pools whose 25% merge reserve (#607/#629) holds one memory merge's copy of 1,000 writes. The `TEMP DEBUG` settings dump, which went to syslog before the log was open and so was never seen, is replaced by three `memory budget` / `memory plan` lines at startup. The image plans for 4 GiB when it has no limit (`ORLY_MEMORY_BUDGET_MB` changes that) and runs in as little as `--memory=768m`. Measured on native arm64: idle 719 MiB at 1g and 2.81 GiB at 4g, both passing the pool-pressure smoke. The docker workflow now runs the image under `--memory=1g` with unlimited memlock, requires it to take 500 writes and read them back without being OOM-killed, and requires the same container with its cgroup hidden to be OOM-killed (#669).
