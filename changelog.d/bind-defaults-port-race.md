- **Fixed**: the listener-defaults smoke could fail in CI with "client listener bound to ''". The smoke before it uses the same ports and ended with `kill -9` without waiting, so the old `orlyi`'s listeners were still open when the readiness check ran: the check passed at once and the sockets were gone a moment later. The smoke now waits up to 30 seconds for all four ports to be free and names the holder if they are not, and the desc-prefix and read-fanout smokes wait for `orlyi` to exit after killing it.
