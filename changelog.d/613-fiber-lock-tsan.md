- **Fixed**: `orly/indy/fiber/fiber_lock.test` no longer times out under ThreadSanitizer (#613). It was slow, not deadlocked: each of its 1,000,000 iterations makes two cross-runner fiber hops for most of its 1,024 fibers, and under TSan that costs about 3 ms per iteration, close to an hour in all against the job's 600 s guard. At 600 s all four runner threads were still on the CPU and gaining CPU time, and smaller counts ran to completion in proportion with no warnings. The TSan build now runs 10,000 iterations (about 25 s); every other build keeps 1,000,000. The test is off the TSan job's known-timeout list, so it gates again.
