- **Fixed**: pausing a POV while its parent's Tetris player was partway through promoting it could abort a debug `orlyi` (`PopLowest`: `Status == Normal`). In release it could promote the paused POV's update anyway, part it from the player twice, and leave the player using a child record it had freed (#636). A pause now waits for any promotion round that has already picked up the POV to finish, and no new round picks the POV up while the pause is pending. Once `pause` returns, nothing more from the POV reaches its parent until `unpause`. The wait is at most one Tetris round. Pausing a player's only child from another thread also no longer races the player's self-destruction, which could throw "Bad file descriptor" out of the commit and terminate `orlyi`.
