- **Added**: a POV review workflow (#746). `diff_pov {<pov>}` lists the keys a POV changed relative to its parent (its unpromoted writes: added, changed and removed keys with the parent's and the POV's values, and for `+=`/`|=` the accumulated delta), optionally by key range (`.start`, `.stop`) and keyset-paged (`.after`, `.limit`). `discard_pov {<pov>}` throws a private POV's unpromoted changes away, so it reads as its parent again, and frees their memory; the discard replicates. A POV made with `<{.conflicts: "report"}>` reports a promotion that overwrites or deletes a key its parent chain changed after the fork, and one made with `"refuse"` is held back instead (`promote_pov {<pov>}` refuses it with the keys, `<{.force: true}>` lets it through); commutative updates never conflict. `review_pov {<pov>}` shows promotion progress and conflicts. All of it is on the WebSocket and binary protocols and in the Python, Go and TypeScript clients, with a GRC-20 walkthrough in `examples/grc20-pov/review.py`; named save points are left for versioned reads (#745). See `docs/pov-review.md`. A discard pops a paused repo, so a repo may now be popped while paused (on a slave too). Tests: `clients/smoke/run-pov-review.sh` (also under TSan), `orly/indy/fork_watch.test`, and `PovReviewOverBinaryProtocol` in `import_replication.test`.
