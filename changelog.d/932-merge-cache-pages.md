- **Fixed**: A disk merge (or a tail) that runs out of cache pages no longer aborts the server: Phase A hands its input layers back and retries after a backoff, and Phase B keeps the valid unfolded Phase A output as it already does on `std::bad_alloc`. `TStream::FetchBuf`, `TStream::PrefetchNextPage`, `TCache::AsyncMultiGet`, `TCache::Release`, and `TDiskArena::TryAcquireNote` also unwind cleanly when `TOutOfCachePages` is thrown so a stream does not release a stale `MainSlot` under the new `LoadedPageId` or leave a cache bucket wedged at `LockedSlot` ([#932](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/932)).
