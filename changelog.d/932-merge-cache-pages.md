- **Fixed**: A disk merge (or a tail) that runs out of cache pages no longer aborts the server. It hands its input layers back and retries after a backoff, as it does when it runs out of disk space or pool space. Before, the `Ran out of cache pages` error reached the catch-all that calls `abort()` ([#932](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/932)).
