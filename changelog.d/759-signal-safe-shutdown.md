- **Fixed**: the SIGINT and SIGTERM handlers that `TScheduler::TPolicy::RunUntilCtrlC` installs (so `orlyi`'s graceful stop) called `Base::ShutDown()` inside the handler, which locks, allocates (an `atexit` registration the first time) and overwrites `errno`, none of it async-signal-safe; ThreadSanitizer reported it on master and could deadlock or corrupt the heap. The handlers now do nothing, and `ShutDown()` runs after `sigsuspend()` returns, in normal context, before the shutdown callback. A second SIGINT or SIGTERM, or the one the scheduler sends itself when it goes quiescent, no longer kills a process that has just shut down cleanly: it is consumed instead of being delivered once the handlers are gone. The daemon's SIGINT/SIGTERM/SIGHUP handler no longer calls `syslog()`. A new `base/scheduler.test` case stops `RunUntilCtrlC` with SIGINT and SIGTERM twenty times; it runs in the TSan job, and on the old code the process died of the leftover SIGTERM (#759).
