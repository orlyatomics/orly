- **Changed (performance)**: A fiber runner now walks its handoff slots only on laps where something was handed to it. Since #1017 it walked every slot on every lap, one load per runner, which cost 1–2% of read throughput with 64 sessions on a quiet server. A pusher now sets a per-runner flag after its push, and the runner swaps the flag off before walking. Parking still checks every slot directly.
