- **Fixed**: A disk merge now looks at the stop flag every 1024 entries while it builds its output file, in the two passes that run over every entry (the note indexes and the in-order key index). Before, a stop that arrived during a large merge waited for that build to reach its next checkpoint, which took seconds under write load. A cancelled merge's exception, and its log line, now name the check that saw the flag ([#1025](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/1025)).
