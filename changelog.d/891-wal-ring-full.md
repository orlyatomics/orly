- **Fixed**: a safe root under sustained write load could hang for good once the WAL ring filled (#891). Ring space was checked only when the WAL leader came to write a record, after its transaction had already been applied, so a record could be queued with no room behind it; the root flush then waited for that record to be durable (write-ahead, #755), the checkpoint that would make room waited for the flush, and the leader waited for the checkpoint. A safe transaction now reserves its record's room in the ring before it applies anything, and the WAL counts every record it has taken but not yet written, so a record it accepts always has room. A client write the ring can't take is refused with `"status": "insufficient_storage"` (retryable, nothing written) and accepted again once a flush and checkpoint free room. Client writes and new POVs leave an eighth of the ring free for the server's own writes (promotion, pause and unpause), so those keep going and drain the backlog. `wal.test` reproduces the hang (it hung on master) and now checks that a full ring refuses rather than strands a record.
