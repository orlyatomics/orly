- **Fixed**: `Util::SleepUntil` never slept. Its comparison was inverted, so it slept only for deadlines already in the past, and since 2014 every interval passed through it has been a no-op. It now sleeps until the deadline, and that matters for three kinds of caller. **The merge-mem and merge-disk loops** still honoured each repo's next-merge deadline (#227), so the broken sleep made them busy-wait for up to 40ms after every merge step. Measured on native arm64 release builds with writes under way, the fix cuts `orlyi`'s CPU by 65–85%, with throughput within run-to-run noise. With one writer, the old build used 10.5–15.5 CPU-seconds per 20s and the fix 1.8–4.6; with eight, 11.6–12.3 against 4.9–5.0 per 15s. **The replication loop and the durable writer and merger** never actually waited out `--replication_interval`, `--durable_write_interval` or `--durable_merge_interval`, and still don't. Their dead sleeps are removed, so turning a real delay on is a measured decision of its own. A working 100ms replication interval, for example, would hold every write back from merging for up to that long, because the loop gates `ReleaseUpdate` even in SOLO. The flags are still accepted. `orly/perf/kv_exercise` now paces its inserts at `FlushIntervalMs`, as it always intended (#576).
