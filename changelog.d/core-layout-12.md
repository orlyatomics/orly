- **Changed**: orlyi on 12-15 CPUs (by affinity mask) now gets a table of its own instead of the 8-CPU one, which left 4 or more CPUs idle and ran 8 disk merge threads on 2 cores: slow runners on 2 cores, memory merges on 2, the disk controller on 1, fast runners on 3, and 4 disk merge threads on 4 cores of their own. In a 64-session ingest pinned to 12 CPUs, this layout settled in 264 s against 454 s for one with 8 disk merge threads on 2 cores, peaked at 371 disk layers against 467, and nearly halved join p50 during ingest and merge. `--num_disk_merge_threads` and the `--*_cores` flags still override it, and the resolved core assignment logged at startup now includes the disk merge thread count.
