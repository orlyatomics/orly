- **Fixed**: the Tetris manager could deadlock the whole server (#657). Commits join and part children under the manager's lock while they hold the replication queue lock, and that lock was a fiber lock: a contended commit parked, and the lock was handed to it on its own runner, which another player's commit could be holding blocked on the replication queue lock. `PausePlayer` (which `BeginImport` uses on the global POV) also waited for the player while holding the manager's lock, so pausing a player in the middle of a round whose commit parts a drained child stopped every commit at once. The manager's lock is now a plain mutex that nothing waits under: `PausePlayer` and `UnpausePlayer` wait for the player outside it, and a player created paused no longer waits inside the joining commit for its first round.
