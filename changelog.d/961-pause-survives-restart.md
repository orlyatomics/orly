- **Fixed**: a paused safe POV came back unpaused after a crash restart once its pause had left the head of the write-ahead log, and Tetris then promoted the updates it was holding back. A pause leaves nothing else behind: the POV's Pov record carries no status, the WAL copied that record forward without the pause, and a POV paused with nothing pending held no update pins, so the checkpoint head moved past the pause. The WAL now keeps the record that paused each live safe POV until it is unpaused, and once that record is half the ring behind it writes a fresh copy of the pause (an ordinary transaction record holding one pause, readable by older binaries) and moves the pin to it, as it does for Pov records. Recovery now discards a replayed pause of a paused repo, or unpause of an unpaused one, instead of asserting, since it can replay a pause and its copy both. `tests/pause_restart_test.sh` (in CI's restart job) pauses POVs, pushes the WAL until their records are copied forward, SIGKILLs and restarts, and requires them paused with their updates unpromoted (#961).
