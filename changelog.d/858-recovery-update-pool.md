- **Fixed**: a `new pov` just after a restart could fail with a bare `bad_alloc` (#858). After a restart, a disk merge could start folding a file larger than the Update pool's free room, taking every free block before it gave up, and the System repo write that makes a new POV's repo then missed. A disk merge's fold now claims the update blocks it needs before it allocates any, and skips the fold, keeping the merge unfolded as it already did on a miss, when the claim would eat into the reserve kept for the merges and promotions that free the pools. A new POV that still finds the Update pool full is refused with the typed, retryable `insufficient_memory` status instead of an untyped error.
- **Documented**: server downtime doesn't count against a POV's TTL: a POV restored by WAL recovery gets its full TTL from the moment recovery completes (#858).
