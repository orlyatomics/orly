- **Added**: write-ahead log library `TWal` (#755 Stage 1). Implements a circular ring buffer on a storage block range with pipelined group commit (dedicated OS leader and syncer threads), group headers, Castagnoli CRC32C per-record checksums, 64-bit header and payload hash chains, piggybacked seals, alternating checkpoints, copy-forward, and crash-recovery scan with torn-tail vs acknowledged-damage classification.
