- **Added**: a kill-and-recover campaign, `tests/kill_campaign.sh`. It SIGKILLs a disk-backed `orlyi` at random moments under write load from eight writers (safe and fast POVs, private and shared, nested POVs, single writes and batches, `+=` counters), restarts it on the same loopback volume 40 times, and checks every write against the client's ledger: per POV a prefix of the commit order survives, batches survive whole, `+=` is applied once, nothing unsent appears, nothing a restart gave back is lost later, the loss stays within what the Update pool held, and the open check passes. CI runs 3 kills and a negative control in the release job; the `kill-campaign` workflow runs the full campaign. `docs/durability.md` states the contract it checks: an acknowledgment, on a safe POV as on a fast one, means the write committed in memory, and it is durable only once promoted to the global POV and written by its memory merge (#730).
