- **Fixed**: the ThreadSanitizer CI gate could not fail on a broken test. Each test ran with `|| true`, and only `WARNING: ThreadSanitizer` lines in the logs were counted, so a test whose EXPECTs failed, that crashed at startup, or that hit the 600 s deadlock guard still passed. A non-zero exit now fails the job. The job also runs a negative control first: `base/tsan_control.test` races two threads on a plain int, and TSan must report it. If that race goes unreported, the instrumentation or the suppressions would hide every other race, so the job fails. The curated set gains the lock-free `base/inv_con/atomic_{ordered,unordered}_list` tests, `base/latch`, `orly/indy/repo` (where #592's pruning merges live) and `orly/indy/transaction_base`.
