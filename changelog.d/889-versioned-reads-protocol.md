- **Added (protocol)**: the versioned reads' statements over WebSocket (#889). `try {pov} at time "<RFC 3339>" ...` reads at the version the retention window's samples give for a time (needs `--retention_seconds`; exact to the sample spacing, the greater of a second and the window over 1024), and `at save "<name>"` reads at a save point's version; both echo the version they read at and are refused wherever `at V` is. `version of {pov};` returns the newest version (a number for the global POV, a vector string otherwise). `save point {pov} "<name>" [at V];` and `drop save point {pov} "<name>";` make and remove save points, which keep their history through merges, survive restarts and failovers on the global POV, and on a POV under review are in-memory names for its newest version that `diff_pov {pov} <{.since: "<name>"}>;` diffs from (until a write after it is promoted). `hold version {pov} V for N;` keeps a version's history for N seconds and `release {lease};` ends it early. **`time`, `save`, `point`, `version`, `of`, `drop`, `hold`, `release` and `for` are now reserved words** in the statement grammar; they stay legal as field names. Not in this change: `keys ... changed between (v1, v2)`, and a `retention` statement (retention stays a server flag). See [`docs/PROTOCOL.md`](docs/PROTOCOL.md#reading-at-a-time-or-a-save-point-889).
