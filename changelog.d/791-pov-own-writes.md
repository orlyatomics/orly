- **Fixed**: a POV now reads its own writes over keys an ancestor POV holds before Tetris promotes them. A read through a POV merges the repos from the POV up to the global POV, and it ordered one key's entries across those repos by sequence number, but each repo numbers its updates itself, so an ancestor's older entry could outrank the POV's newer one. Until the write was promoted, a point read, `is known` and a `keys` walk through the POV, or through any child of it, still saw the ancestor's value after a delete or an overwrite, and a `+=` on an ancestor's counter could be dropped or folded onto the wrong base. Paused POVs kept that window open indefinitely. The merge now prefers the repo nearest the POV for each key. `orlyc`'s test runner no longer needs to seed each test POV's sequence counter from its parent to get the same effect, so that workaround is gone. A `context_xrepo.test` case and a new smoke, `clients/smoke/run-pov-own-writes.sh` (safe and fast, shared and private POVs, under a paused shared POV and under the global POV), cover deletes, overwrites, a delete and re-put, and `+=` (#791).
