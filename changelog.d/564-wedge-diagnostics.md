- **Changed**: `import_replication.test`'s `GracefulShutdownUnresponsiveSlave` now interrogates the master before asserting on a failed `Reap()`, dumping each thread's state, kernel wait channel and `syscall` line. A wedged graceful shutdown is otherwise undiagnosable after the fact: the only syslog between the connection drain and `TServer::Shutdown() complete` is the drain's own deadline warning, so a wedge anywhere in between looks identical from the log tails, and the process is dead by the time anyone reads CI. Two aarch64 occurrences have already been lost that way (#564).
