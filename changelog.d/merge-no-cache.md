- **Fixed**: `TOutStream::Flush` ignored its `DoCache` member and always passed `Disk::Util::CacheAll` to `WriteBlock`, and `TMergeDataFile` and `TFoldDataFile` passed `do_cache = true` / `CacheAll` on all merge writes. `TOutStream::Flush` now passes `DoCache ? CacheAll : ClearAll`, and `TMergeDataFile` and `TFoldDataFile` (via `TDataFile`) write with `do_cache = false` and `ClearAll` so disk merges drop stale cached pages/blocks for reused blocks without filling the page and block caches with cold merged output.
