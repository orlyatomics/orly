- **Fixed**: a promoted slave could lose the exactly-once entry for a write it applied from its master. The slave appends the replicated record to its own log before `RestoreOperation` puts the entry in its table, and nothing held the checkpoint head in between, so a checkpoint there could pass the record with the entry in neither the snapshot nor replay; a retry after the promoted slave crashed and restarted ran the write twice. The slave now holds the head at the record's WAL position from before the append until the entry is in the table, the same floor a master's committing write takes (#861). `tests/failover_retry_test.py` now runs its slave disk-backed, with the log (#866).
