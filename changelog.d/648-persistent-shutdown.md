- **Fixed**: `orlyi` on disk volumes (not `--mem_sim`) no longer aborts or hangs on SIGINT/SIGTERM. Two separate bugs. (1) The file service's background loop runs on a job of the scheduler that `RunUntilCtrlC` destroys before `~TServer` runs, and nothing stopped it during `Shutdown()`. The scheduler's teardown interrupted it (`EINTR` escaping the runner fiber), gave up after ten seconds, and `~TFileService` then locked the dead scheduler's mutex and aborted: every disk-backed shutdown exited 134 about 11 s after `Shutdown() complete`. `Shutdown()` now stops and joins the file service while the scheduler is alive. (2) The Tetris runners stayed in the list of runners that `ForEachScheduler` visits after the Tetris manager was deleted. When tearing down the repo manager removed a disk layer's file, `TSafeRepo::RemoveFile` switched to a dead runner and never came back, so shutdown hung forever (about 1 run in 5 after heavy connection churn). `tests/restart_test.sh` now also fails on a non-zero exit or an abort after `Shutdown() complete` (#648).
