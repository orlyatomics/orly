- **Changed (performance)**: a stream's read-ahead no longer pins and releases pages that are already cached. `TCache::AsyncMultiGet` checks each end of a prefetch run without taking the slot lock, and drops pages that are already loaded or loading before taking any pin; each skipped page saves two slot-lock CASes and an LRU remove and insert under the LRU spinlock. In a mixed-read benchmark with concurrent ingest (ABBA, 2 runs per side) closed-loop reads rise about 1.4% quiet and 2.1% under ingest. Resident pages are no longer moved up the LRU by a prefetch; demand reads still move them (#1046).
