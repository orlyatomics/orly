- **Changed**: A graceful stop now logs how long each of its phases took (the connection drain, the Tetris wait, the settle sleep, the replication stop, the housekeeper and scrub joins, the merge runners, the WAL checkpointer, the flush, the layer cleaner, the teardown and the runner stop) at `LOG_INFO`. Stopping the merge runners logs when each disk merge loop exits after the stop flag, and a cancelled disk merge logs how long it ran before it saw the flag and how long its unwind took. The "merge runners stopped after" and "flushed after" lines gave only the time since the drain began, which made a slow stop look like a slow merge stop ([#1025](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/1025)).
- **Fixed**: A fold now checks the stop flag while it writes its arenas, between its two walks of the source and before its meta write, instead of only inside the source. Under write load the merge-runner phase of a stop fell from 6.9–57.6 s to 1.6–7.0 s in three runs each ([#1025](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/1025)).
