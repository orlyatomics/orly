- **Fixed**: a release server that lost power while folding a disk merge, before the #666 fix, could restart onto a merge output holding two copies of one key, and read that key's `+=` history wrong until a later merge folded the file. Within one disk file, two keys are taken to differ when they sit at different places in the file, and the two copies do. Key equality checks now compare the two values in full when that is the only difference and the keys' stored hashes match; without duplicates this happens only on a hash collision. Key ordering is unchanged. `clients/smoke/run-read-fold.sh` is a new read-heavy workload for `ab-bench` (#674).
