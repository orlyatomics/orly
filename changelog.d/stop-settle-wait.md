- **Changed (performance)**: A graceful stop no longer sleeps a fixed 300 ms before it stops the replication loops. It waits until every release a promotion owes its repos (issued before the stop) has run, bounded by the old 300 ms, and logs how long that took. A write that arrived just before the signal is still made mergeable first, and the shutdown flush is unchanged; an idle or lightly loaded server stops about 300 ms sooner. (#1025)
