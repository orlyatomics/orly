- **Changed**: a disk merge whose merged file is larger than the new `--max_fold_input_mb` keeps its output unfolded instead of folding it, as it already did when a fold ran out of memory (#627), unless a key in it has more than 64 entries that are not assignments. Those files still fold, so no key's `+=` run grows past that. A fold holds its whole output in memory, about three times the merged file's size, and one large fold was the biggest part of the peak heap under a heavy read load. The cap defaults to a sixteenth of the memory budget (384 MiB at 6 GiB); `--max_fold_input_mb=0` turns it off. With a 6 GiB memory budget and a read ladder of 1 to 256 sessions over about 400 disk files, peak RSS went from 7.45 to 7.97 GiB over five runs to 5.74 to 5.93 GiB over four, reads per second at 64 and 256 sessions went up 12 to 21%, and the runs finished 100 to 103 disk merges instead of 83 to 85. A read of a key with a `+=` run in a merged file over the cap walks that run, at most 64 entries (#988).
