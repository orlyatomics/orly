- **Fixed**: a slave now builds each repo it learns of when it joins with the ttl, parent and safety the master sent. It used to open each one with `GetRepo(..., create=false)`, which ignores all three, so every such repo came out safe, with no parent and a 1000 s ttl. After a failover, a pov made under a shared pov that existed at the join read through to nothing instead of reaching its parent and global, and fast povs had become safe. The master now inventories each repo after its parent, including a parent with no ttl, which has no saved-repo entry of its own. The slave builds each repo under its copy of the parent, and never builds a stand-in for a parent it lacks. A repo the replication stream creates during the join, under a parent the inventory hasn't built yet, now waits until the inventory has built it, instead of getting an empty stand-in as its parent (#676).
