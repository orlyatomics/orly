- **Fixed (latency)**: an idle fiber runner no longer polls its queues with 10 µs sleeps. It spins briefly as before, then parks on a futex that a push onto its queues (or `ShutDown()`) wakes, so a request hopping onto an idle runner pays a wakeup instead of a timer tick: a tick is ~60 µs on bare Linux and ~3 ms in a Docker VM on macOS, where an idle server's one-key read took ~6 ms. The disk controller's queue runner (`TDiskController::QueueRunner`) parks the same way when it has no I/O in flight. A park also wakes on a 100 ms safety-net timeout. New: `Base::TParker`, the `HopOntoParkedRunner` and `ShutDownWakesParkedRunner` fiber tests, and `clients/smoke/run-point-read.sh`, which reports idle-server read latency percentiles, startup time and idle CPU as METRIC lines and runs in CI with a loose median ceiling that catches a lost wakeup (#764).
