- **Fixed**: a graceful stop (SIGTERM or SIGINT, so `docker stop`) under write load usually hung, and when it finished it could lose acknowledged writes. Four causes, all in the shutdown order: `TServer::Shutdown()` waited for the merge runners on a fast runner's fiber, while a disk merge that had just folded needed to visit that runner to drop a removed file's caches, a deadlock; it stopped the repo and durable layer cleaners before its flush, so the repo pools ran dry (the flush gave up after 30 s and dropped what it hadn't written) and so did the Durable Layer pool (the bad_alloc killed the durable writer fiber, and every saver waiting on it hung); and it flushed without waiting for Tetris to promote the POVs' backlogs, so writes promoted after the flush were dropped at exit. Now the merge runners are stopped, and the repo cleaner joined, from `Shutdown()`'s own thread; each cleaner stops only once nothing makes dead layers for it; and `Shutdown()` waits, bounded at 30 s, for every unpaused Tetris player to finish before the release machinery stops and the flush runs. Also: the durable writer and merger take their layers from the pool before writing, retry when it is dry instead of dying, and never let an exception out of their fibers; the durable manager frees its last mapping's layers instead of leaking them at exit; a fold groups keys through a hash map instead of a linear scan (it was O(entries x keys), minutes of CPU for a file of a few tens of thousands of keys, and a stop waits for the merge in flight). A SIGINT the scheduler sends itself during a long shutdown no longer makes a clean exit die of SIGINT either; that fix is shared with #759. `orlyi` logs how long each step of a stop took. A new CI smoke, `tests/graceful_stop_test.sh`, stops `orlyi` four times under the kill campaign's write load and requires each stop to finish within 60 s and every acknowledged write back; before the fix it hung on most stops and lost acknowledged writes. `docs/durability.md` and `docs/teardown-design.md` describe the new order (#744).
