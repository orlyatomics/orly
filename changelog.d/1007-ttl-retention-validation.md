- **Fixed**: a negative `set ttl` over the WebSocket protocol is now refused with an `exception` reply up front, so it cannot reach the assert in `TObj::SetTtl` once `TSession::SetTimeToLive` is re-enabled. `--retention_seconds` above 4000000000 (about 127 years) is now a usage error at startup, and the retention window is capped in both places that converted it to nanoseconds (the time-resolution sampler and the retention sweep), where a value above about 9.2e9 overflowed (#1007).
