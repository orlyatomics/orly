- **Changed (performance)**: A range read spends about 3% fewer cycles building its result ([#1018](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/1018)). When the elements of a long list propose notes of their own, as the rows of a result do, the in-memory arena now makes room for all of them when it sees the first one. Before, its note interner grew a step at a time and rehashed every note it held at each step. With 64 sessions running 1,000-row integer ranges on 14 fast cores, cycles per read on those cores fell from 2.92M to 2.83M and instructions per read from 6.54M to 6.17M, in A B B A order. Paged reads were unchanged.
