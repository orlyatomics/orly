- **Changed (performance)**: A point read no longer waits behind a long scan on the same fast runner ([#1015](https://github.com/orlyatomics/orly/issues/1015)). A method call that has run for 500 µs without a break (`--read_yield_us`) yields to another statement that is ready to run on its runner, so a short read waits about that long behind a scan rather than for all of it, and a call shorter than that never yields. Time a call spends waiting for disk doesn't count toward the 500 µs, and it doesn't yield to statements that are themselves waiting for disk. It looks at the clock every 8 rows it walks (`--read_yield_rows`; a sequence's elements count as 1/64 of a row), and as it walks it adds to its runner's load, one small statement's worth per 32 rows, so new statements steer around a runner that is busy with a scan. `--read_yield_us=0` turns off the yields; `--read_yield_rows=0` turns off both.
