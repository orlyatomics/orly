- **Fixed (undefined behavior, found by a UBSan build)**: the disk stream buffers (`TOutStream::Write` and `TStream::Read` for 16, 32 and 64-bit integers) stored and loaded through `reinterpret_cast` pointers at arbitrary byte offsets, which are misaligned for the type. They now use `memcpy`, which compiles to the same single move on x86-64 and AArch64; the unpadded on-disk format is unchanged (#992). `TUuid::GetHash` likewise read its first eight bytes through a misaligned, type-punned `size_t *`, and now copies them (#994).
