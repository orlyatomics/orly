- **Fixed**: `TRangeGenerator` computed `limit - start` (and `second - start` for the stride) in signed 64-bit arithmetic, which overflows, and is undefined behaviour, for a range that spans more than `INT64_MAX`, such as `[INT64_MIN, INT64_MAX)`: that range got a stride of -1 and was refused or empty. The direction is now picked by comparing the ends, the stride is held in 128 bits so a step across the whole int64 range keeps its value, and a cursor whose next step would leave the int64 range ends there instead of overflowing past `INT64_MAX` or `INT64_MIN`. `generator.test` covers ranges at both ends of int64, empty and one-element ranges, strides wider than `INT64_MAX`, and reversed ranges. (`take` and `skip` only compare counts, so they had nothing to fix.) (#805).
